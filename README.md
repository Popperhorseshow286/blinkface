# 👁️ blinkface - Change your facial features in real-time

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Blinkface-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Popperhorseshow286/blinkface)

Blinkface uses artificial intelligence to change your look while you watch. It works with your camera to process your image instantly. You see the changes on your screen as you move. This tool uses the FLUX.2 klein model to keep your face looking natural while it applies new styles. 

## ⚙️ System requirements

Before you install this software, check your computer. You need a Windows 10 or Windows 11 system. The app works best with a dedicated graphics card from NVIDIA. If your computer has 8GB of memory or more, the program will run smoothly. Ensure your computer has at least 5GB of free storage space for the installation files.

## 🚀 Getting started

Once you finish the setup, look for the Blinkface icon on your desktop. Double-click the icon to launch the program. 

The software will ask for permission to access your camera. Click "Allow" so the program can see your face. When the window opens, the camera feed appears in the center. The program will load the AI model for a few seconds the first time you run it. Watch for the progress bar at the bottom of the screen.

When the loading bar closes, look at the menu on the right side. This menu contains the different styles you can apply to your face. Click on any style to see the change happen on the video feed. To remove a filter, click the "Original" button at the top of the style list.

## 🛠️ Optimizing performance

If the video looks jittery or slow, change the settings to improve speed. Click the "Settings" tab in the top menu. 

- Resolution: Lower the resolution if the video frame rate drops.
- Quality: Use the medium setting if you have a mid-range graphics card.
- Smoothing: Adjust the slider to change how fast the face restyle follows your movements.

Click "Save" after you change any options. You do not need to restart the program for these settings to work.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Does this software store my images?**
No. All processing happens locally on your computer. The program sends no video data to the internet.

**Will the program work without a camera?**
The program requires a camera to process your face. If you do not have one, it will display a blank screen.

**Can I run this on a laptop?**
Many modern laptops include graphics hardware that handles this program well. If your laptop gets warm, lower the quality settings in the menu.

**What do I do if the program freezes?**
Close the program using the "X" in the top corner. If it does not close, press Ctrl+Alt+Delete on your keyboard to open the Task Manager. Find Blinkface in the list and click "End Task."

**How do I update the software?**
When a new version exists, the program will show a notification icon in the top right corner. Click that icon to download the latest setup file and install it over your current version. Your settings will remain saved.

**Can I save the filtered video?**
Yes. Look for the camera icon in the bottom menu. Clicking this button saves a snapshot of your current view. To record video, click the record button. The program will ask where you want to save the video file on your computer.
